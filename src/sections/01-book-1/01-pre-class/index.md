Remember, your learning is your responsibility. We'll give you the structure, the exercises and the support, but the questions have to come from you. Ask them early and ask them often.

### "Why Not ChatGPT?"

Chances are you already have a favorite chatbot. Maybe it's ChatGPT, maybe it's Gemini because it came with your Google account, or maybe it's whatever your company rolled out last spring. So the first fair question is, why are we asking you to sign up for Claude? The short answer is that we need everyone in the room looking at the same screen. The longer answer is worth a few minutes, because it explains how this whole course is built.

Think back to learning to drive. You probably learned in one car, whatever your family had in the driveway. You learned where the mirrors were, how hard to press the brake and how wide to take a turn. Then one day you rented a car on a trip, spent a minute hunting for the headlight switch and drove off. You didn't relearn driving. Checking your mirrors, judging distance and reading the road all came with you. Only the buttons were in different places.

That's what we're doing here. Claude is the car we learn in. The skills you'll build in this course, such as writing a clear request, giving a tool the right background and checking its work before you trust it, are the driving. They carry over to any AI tool you use, now or five years from now. In other words, learn the craft, not the buttons.

### What We Use in Class

<!-- CHECK: name, feature | checked 2026-10-20 | https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude, https://claude.com/pricing, https://gemini.google/subscriptions/ -->
**Claude Pro, for the whole course.** Claude is made by a company called Anthropic, and it's the one tool you'll use in every session, from your first request in Session 1 to the agents in Book 3. We chose it for a few practical reasons:

- **One app for the whole journey** - Claude lets you save standing instructions about yourself, keep related work together in `projects`, build interactive pages and dashboards (Claude calls these `artifacts`) and connect to your email and calendar. Its agent features, which used to be a separate tool called Claude Cowork, now live in the same app. You won't have to switch tools when the course moves from chatbots to agents.
- **The paid plan covers what we need** - the free plan is good, but several exercises need more usage, more capable models or features that only come with Pro.
- **Everyone sees the same thing** - when you're stuck, your instructor and your group can see exactly what you're seeing, which makes it much easier to help.

Keep in mind, this isn't a claim that Claude is the best at everything. No tool is, and the rankings shift every few months. It's simply the tool that lets us teach the whole course without changing horses midstream.

**Gemini Notebook, for one session.** In Session 4 we'll do research two ways. First we'll use Claude, which draws on what it learned in training plus what it finds on the web. Then we'll use Gemini Notebook, a free Google tool that answers *only* from documents you give it and cites the passage it used. Seeing the two side by side is one of the best ways to learn when an answer deserves your trust. You'll need a Google account, and the free plan is enough. If you've heard of NotebookLM, it's the same tool under a new name.

### What You May Already Use

If you use ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok or Microsoft Copilot at work or at home, keep using them. Each one has its own version of the same ideas: standing instructions, memory, projects and connections to your other apps. The names change, the ideas don't.

In fact, knowing a second tool is an advantage. Later in Book 1 you'll put the same question to two different sources and work out why the answers differ. If you already know another tool, you have a built-in comparison. Just do the class exercises in Claude, so your results match what we're discussing.

### What Comes Later

<!-- CHECK: name | checked 2026-10-20 | https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507, https://zapier.com/mcp, tool-dashboard (ChatGPT Work and Grok Bot cards) -->
Book 1 is about the chatbot: getting good at asking, and getting good at checking. Books 2 and 3 move on to `agents`, tools that take steps on your behalf instead of just answering. Session 1 explains the difference properly. For now, here's who you'll meet along the way:

- **Claude, again** - Claude's agent features are the hands-on tool for Books 2 and 3. Claude can work through a task with several steps, use your files and connected apps and run work on a schedule. All of this comes with Claude Pro.
- **Zapier** - a service that links thousands of business apps together. In Book 2 you'll use it to reach apps that Claude can't connect to on its own.
- **`MCP` connectors** - MCP is a common standard for plugging AI tools into other software, a bit like a universal travel adapter. Book 2 explains how it works.
- **Grok Bot** - a different style of agent, where a team of always-on agents works for you in the cloud. You'll read about it at the end of Book 1, and it comes back later in the course.
- **ChatGPT Work and Gemini's Tasks** - the agents from OpenAI and Google. (Google first called its agent Spark.) You'll read about them as points of comparison, but you won't need either one.
