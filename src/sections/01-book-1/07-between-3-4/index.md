## Goals

By the end of this assignment, you will be able to:

- **List** the repetitive workflows in your own week
- **Describe** each one clearly enough that someone else could follow it
- **Judge** which ones are worth handing to AI, and which ones need you

## Take Inventory

Take a deeper look at your week. Where does your time actually go? Most of us can name the big projects, but the hours tend to leak out through the small, repeated tasks that nobody thinks of as work: the weekly report, the follow-up email, the invoice reminder and copying numbers from one place to another.

1. For three working days, jot down every task you do more than once. Don't filter yet.
2. Pick the five that eat the most time or that you dread the most.
3. For each one, write a few lines: What starts it? What are the steps? What does "done" look like? Where do you have to make a judgment call?
4. Mark each one: **good fit** (repetitive, rule-based, easy to check), **partial fit** (some steps are, some aren't) or **keep it human** (mostly judgment or relationships).

You're done when you have five workflows written down and marked, and you can explain why you marked each one the way you did.
