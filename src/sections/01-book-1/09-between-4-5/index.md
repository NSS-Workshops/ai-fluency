<summary>Proposed homework: The Second Read (about 30 minutes)</summary>

<div class="crafts">

**Crafts in this assignment:** Review *(Exercise)*

</div>

**Why here:** In the craft tree, **Review** is where three checking threads meet: the final read before sending (Session 3), checking a summary against its source (Session 4) and tracing a number (the homework between 2 and 3). It needs its own time, and homework is the cheapest place to give it that. It also has to come before anything that saves a prompt to run again, which is what Skills do.

### Goals

- **Run** a deliberate second read on AI output, aimed at catching errors
- **Combine** the checks you've learned into one routine you can repeat

### Build Your Checklist

So far you've checked AI output three different ways. You proofread a draft before it went out. You compared a summary to its source. You traced a number back to where it came from. Now let's turn those into one habit.

1. Write a short checklist, five items at most, of the checks you'd run on any important piece of AI output.
2. Pick one piece of AI output from this course that you haven't checked closely yet.
3. Run your checklist on it. Note what each check caught, or that it caught nothing.
4. Revise the checklist. Did one check do all the work? Was one a waste of time?
5. Post your final checklist in Slack and compare it with your classmates'.

You're done when you have a checklist you've actually used, and revised based on what it caught.
